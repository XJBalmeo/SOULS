# souls-boss-design

Read AGENTS.md and current milestone first.

## Procedure
Honor fixed chapter order and distinct physical forms. Propose two or three readable attacks, arena and counterplay, health bar and nearby checkpoint. Approve art/attacks; implement full HP retry and victory transition; test defeat/retry.

## Deliverables
Boss spec, assets, playable encounter, progression evidence.

## Gate
Review actual outputs, relevant tests and licenses; record limits in docs/DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md. No unapproved scope changes or false completion claims.

## v1.1 — Parry Death
Parry Death must work during every boss, including Chapter IV; successful revival preserves boss HP and encounter progress. Distinguish special Death enemy from Horseman.
