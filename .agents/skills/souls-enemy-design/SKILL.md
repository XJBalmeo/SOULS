# souls-enemy-design

Read AGENTS.md and current milestone first.

## Procedure
Use ENEMY_ROSTER and ENEMY_SPEC. Inspect existing rat; choose unique role, silhouette, HP, attack windows and reset behavior. Approve concept before art. Reuse melee/ranged/flying/heavy/support behavior. Coordinate sprite, SFX, LDtk and QA.

## Deliverables
Approved spec, integrated enemy and tests.

## Gate
Review actual outputs, relevant tests and licenses; record limits in docs/DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md. No unapproved scope changes or false completion claims.

## v1.1 — Parry Death
Death’s Shadow is a special once-per-campaign encounter, not a regular enemy or final boss; one approach and strike, distinct silhouette. Read docs/PARRY_DEATH.md.
