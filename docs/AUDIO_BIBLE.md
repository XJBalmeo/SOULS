# Audio direction
Proposed Famine: sparse strings, hollow drones, wind, dripping water and rat noises. War: percussion/brass; Conquest: regal orchestration/choir textures; Death: spectral drones and climactic score. Menu theme and each chapter exploration/boss music, ending music; distinct combat/movement/UI/enemy effects. ACE-Step for instrumental candidates, jsfxr for procedural effects, licensed sources or Stable Audio Open for organic effects after license review. Use FFmpeg/Audacity to trim, normalize, loop and export browser-compatible audio; test formats on target browsers. Separate music/SFX settings, audio unlock after player gesture, transitions and volume balance. Save generation prompts/seed/model, source and license. No runtime generation dependency. Listen to actual output and verify loops; do not claim audio is approved solely from a prompt.

Parry Death: distinct approach/telegraph/strike/parry/revive/failure cues; respect volume and pause; audible warning supplements readable visual warning. Restore prior music on revival.
